# CargoGo 1.3.1 — Disputes

- Dispute opening freezes non-settled settlement into manual review.
- Each side can add notes and up to 3 camera photos (JPEG/PNG, max 10 MB each).
- Existing pickup/delivery evidence is attached automatically.
- Admin-only dispute queue with claim/review/resolve flow.
- Evidence stays private and is exposed only through 5-minute signed URLs with access logging.
- Resolution requires a written reason and notifies both parties.
- Deliberately no chat-like bureaucracy, appeals tree, scoring engine, or automatic AI decisions in this version.
